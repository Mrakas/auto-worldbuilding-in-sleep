# Seaside Boulevard Preset

Apply this preset when the user requests a GTA VI trailer-inspired coastal boulevard. These are configurable task defaults, not requirements for every Blender scene. The attached user reference is the main visual target. Official trailer frames may supply secondary research references; record their source and timestamps, and do not redistribute them without appropriate rights. No game assets or reference images are bundled with this skill.

## Scope and defaults

- Blender only; refine the existing boulevard rather than expanding into unrelated trailer locations.
- Preserve approximately 250 meters of avenue, eight hotels/shops, palms and planted areas, a promenade, beach, ocean, and distant skyline.
- Preserve the original delivery package and baseline scene.
- Fixed ten-hour run: nine hours fifteen minutes of iteration, then forty-five minutes for regression fixes, final recording, and packaging.
- Fifteen-minute heartbeat, one active instance, and a sleep inhibitor where supported.
- Twenty GiB ceiling for the agreed combined existing/new project data, with at least eight GiB physical free space. Measure the baseline before starting; clarify accounting if the input already exceeds the ceiling.
- Chinese round feedback, operating instructions, and final report unless the user requests another language. The skill itself is written in English for portability.

## Reference interpretation

Target a believable humid coastal dusk: cool blue-violet sky, a warm horizon, localized amber interiors, restrained cyan and pink neon, wet pavement, detailed hotel fronts, slender railings, layered palms, and a readable ocean edge. Preserve depth and plausible light sources rather than applying a global color filter.

### Wet road and water

Distinguish asphalt grain, repairs, worn paint, damp patches, thin water films, and standing puddles. Vary roughness at appropriate scales. Reflections should break with surface relief and connect to real windows, lamps, signs, and sky. Avoid uniformly mirror-like pavement, oily plastic sheen, repeated puddle patterns, and floating rectangular highlights. Check grazing and eye-level views.

Give the sea coherent wave scale, shoreline transitions, depth cues, and dusk reflections. Avoid using identical water behavior for road puddles and the ocean.

### Light and depth

Balance cool ambient sky with local warm shop windows, streetlights, and neon spill. Preserve readable shaded structure, contact shadows, facade depth, and distance haze. Avoid flooding every surface with fill light, hiding defects through underexposure, or letting bloom replace illumination. Check actual surface contribution from emissive fixtures, exposure consistency, and temporal stability.

### Architecture and small structures

Prioritize balcony and promenade railings, thin bars, handrails, joints, fixing plates, frames, window recesses, glass, curtains, and visible interior depth. Develop awnings, doors and handles, signs, lamp housings, plaster variation, bevels, weathering, curb edges, drainage, paving joints, and road markings where the camera can resolve them. Verify plausible dimensions, structural attachment, and contact. More repeated objects do not automatically mean more realism.

### Vegetation and setting

Improve palm bark, frond silhouette, leaf variation, translucency, and shadow structure. Vary plant height, density, species, and color without obscuring the street layout. Avoid evenly spaced identical silhouettes and flat plant cards visible from the tour. Keep sky, haze, skyline, coastline, and sea lighting coherent.

### People, vehicles, and navigation

Preserve existing people, vehicles, animations, and any navigation controls. Inspect grounding, sliding, turns, camera intersections, wheel motion, and lights when present. Verify existing collision, boundaries, reset behavior, and safe exit where those mechanisms exist. List absent capabilities rather than claiming Blender animation implements gameplay. Do not add a separate engine to satisfy earlier superseded engine requirements.

## Coverage and final media

Rotate tours through the avenue, facades, shopfronts, railings, low wet-road views, planting, and promenade. Maintain a fixed avenue hero camera for comparison.

Deliver at least six final native stills covering the wide avenue, wet pavement/reflections, building/rail details, shopfront lighting, vegetation, and promenade/ocean. Include an approximately thirty-second final native tour, matched baseline/final comparisons, and a local gallery for browsing images and videos. All gallery media must resolve from the delivery package.
